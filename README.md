Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty **"The One-Line Cat Joke That Lands"** — one single-line joke about cats, under 200 characters.

Running `main.ts` (Node 22) writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My cat isn't knocking things off the table out of spite — he's running gravity tests, and so far every glass has failed the exam.

The joke turns on a fresh angle: the cat isn't being spiteful, it's a scientist — and every glass keeps failing its gravity exam.
